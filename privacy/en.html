Carrier Jam Privacy Policy

RED GEMS ("we") publishes the mobile game Carrier Jam (캐리어 잼) (the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game handles and why, in line with the Korean Personal Information Protection Act and Google Play policies.

1. Summary

  • The Game has no accounts. We do not ask you for your name, phone number or email.
  • Your progress (levels, coins, settings) is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Google collects some information automatically, such as device identifiers, to serve ads (Google AdMob) and process purchases (Google Play Billing).
  • When you send a challenge after an endless run ("KakaoTalk" or "Send challenge" on the results), a picture and a link with your score (and your rank at that moment) and the profile you chose in the Game (picture, frame and title) go to the app you pick (KakaoTalk or any other). The profile travels only as numbers of the Game's own pictures; no name or other personal information is included, and nothing goes through our servers. When you open a challenge link from a friend, only that score is kept on your device, and on first launch the Game checks Google Play's install information once (whether the install came from a challenge link).
  • Friends (swapping hearts) use a random friend code made on your device. Your friends list and the names you give your friends are stored only on your device; invite and heart links carry only friend codes, a date and the profile you chose in the Game (as picture numbers), and go to the app you pick. Nothing goes through our servers.
  • Reminders (hearts full, the daily gift) are shown only if you turn them on. They are scheduled on your device, at most one a day, and send nothing to us; you can turn them off in Settings at any time.
  • The Game may open Google Play's rating card. Ratings and reviews go to Google Play; the Game itself receives nothing about them.

3. How long we keep information

  • Progress stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • Information collected by Google is kept under Google's retention policies.
  • Support emails are kept for 1 year after we answer, then deleted, unless a law requires longer.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or give your personal information to third parties, except that the Google services in section 5 collect information directly within the Game, and where the law requires disclosure.

6. Personalised ads

  • The Game may show personalised ads based on your advertising ID and app activity.
  • You can opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads by deleting your advertising ID (Android 12 and later) or turning on "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (earlier versions). Resetting the ID creates a new one, which breaks the link to past activity.
  • Players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see a consent form on first launch and can change their choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy choices.

7. Deletion

We delete information without delay once its retention period ends or its purpose is met. Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered, and support emails are permanently removed from our mailbox.

8. Your rights

  • You can ask us at any time to access, correct, delete or stop processing your personal information. Contact us using the details below.
  • You can delete progress stored on your device with Settings > Reset progress, or by uninstalling the Game.
  • You can manage information collected by Google in your Google Account settings and as described in section 6.

9. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that we have, we delete it promptly.

10. Security

  • We do not run our own servers that store player information.
  • Information collected by Google SDKs is encrypted in transit with TLS.
  • Our support mailbox is protected with two-step verification.

11. Automatic collection

The Game does not use web cookies. The advertising ID in section 2 serves a similar role; you can limit it as described in section 6.
