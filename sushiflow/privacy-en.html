SushiFlow Privacy Policy

RED GEMS ("the developer") provides the mobile game "SushiFlow" ("the game") and handles users' personal information in accordance with applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea, and Google Play policies. This policy explains what information the game handles and how.

1. Summary

  • The game has no sign-up and does not ask you for your name, phone number, or email.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, coins, items, stickers, event records, settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer.
  • Opponents in Race and Lava Challenge ("animal guests") are computer characters running on your device; no data is exchanged with other players.
  • For advertising (Google AdMob), Google automatically collects some information such as device identifiers.
  • This version has no in-app purchases. If purchases are added, this policy will be updated first.

3. Retention

  • Progress stored on your device: deleted immediately when you uninstall the game or clear its app data.
  • Information collected by Google: retained according to Google's retention policies.
  • Support emails: kept for 1 year after we reply, then deleted, unless a law requires longer retention.

4. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not sell or provide your personal information to third parties, except that the Google service in section 5 collects information directly within the game, and where required by law. Some U.S. state laws may treat Google's collection of the Advertising ID for personalized ads as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information; you can opt out as described in section 6.

6. Personalized ads

  • The game may show personalized ads using the Advertising ID and app usage.
  • You can opt out in Android Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads by deleting your Advertising ID (Android 12+) or turning on "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (older versions). Resetting the ID creates a new one and breaks the link to past records.
  • Residents of U.S. states with privacy laws (e.g., California) can opt out of the "sale/sharing" of personal information for personalized ads in the same way.
  • The game is not distributed in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland.

7. Deletion

Information is deleted without delay when the retention period ends or the purpose is achieved. Electronic files are deleted irrecoverably and support emails are permanently deleted from the mailbox.

8. Your rights (data deletion requests)

  • You may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of your personal information at any time by contacting us below.
  • To request deletion, email redgemspro@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". The only information the developer holds directly is support email, which we delete promptly upon request (unless retention is legally required, see section 3).
  • Progress on your device can be deleted by uninstalling the game or via Android Settings > Apps > SushiFlow > Storage > Clear data.
  • Information collected by Google can be managed in your Google Account settings and as described in section 6.

9. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 (Google Play target age 13+; under 14 in Korea) and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that such information was collected, we will delete it immediately.

10. Security

  • The developer does not store user information on any server.
  • Information collected by the Google SDK is encrypted in transit with TLS.
  • Support email is managed only in an account protected by two-step verification.
